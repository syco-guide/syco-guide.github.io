    What this page does

    Plan a study walks through the decisions in the paper: what sycophancy targets, whether it must be triggered, whether a ground truth exists, and whether the LM knows the truth, and which metric type fits. It lists the dimensions from the literature for each choice, studies and benchmarks with the same scope, and what to report.

    Meta-ranking combines the rankings of the literature: it fits a Plackett–Luce model on the selected rankings in the browser. An LM has a consistent position if it appears in at least the minimum number of rankings and the 95% interval of its score, from resampling the rankings, lies entirely below or above 0. Ties are broken at random.

    Check a ranking computes Kendall's τ between a ranking and the order of the Plackett–Luce model, on the LMs both share.

    Direct-ranking shows our own evaluation of 18 LMs on 14 datasets from Beacon, ELEPHANT, SYCON, syco-bench, SycophancyEval, and a replication of PARROT on MMLU. Predictions lets you download any subset of its responses and labels.

    Prototype. The model is fitted with a minorisation–maximisation algorithm and a weak prior, not with the exact estimator of the paper, so scores can differ slightly from the paper. Positions are an order, not a level of sycophancy.
